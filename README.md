# GJ Federation: Connecting Game Jolt to the Fediverse

[![Community Link](https://img.shields.io/badge/Join_the_Community-Game_Jolt-green?style=for-the-badge)](https://gamejolt.com/c/Random-t5hewm)

## 🌐 Our Vision
**GJ Federation** is a community-driven initiative dedicated to advocating for the integration of the **ActivityPub** protocol into Game Jolt. 

Our goal is not to make Game Jolt open-source, but to follow the path of modern platforms like Meta's **Threads**. By adopting ActivityPub, Game Jolt would become part of the **Fediverse**, allowing seamless interaction between Game Jolt users and millions of others on decentralized platforms such as Mastodon, Lemmy, and PixelFed.

---

## 🚀 Why ActivityPub for Game Jolt?

By joining the Fediverse, Game Jolt would unlock a new era of growth for creators and gamers:

* **Cross-Platform Visibility:** Your posts on Game Jolt could be seen and followed by users on other platforms (e.g., Mastodon) without them needing a Game Jolt account.
* **Boosted Engagement:** Likes, boosts (shares), and comments from external Fediverse instances would sync back to your Game Jolt posts, significantly increasing your reach.
* **Universal Following:** A user on a different server could "follow" your Game Jolt profile just like they follow anyone else, bringing more eyes to your indie games and content.
* **Interoperability:** It positions Game Jolt as a leader in the future of the social web, emphasizing connectivity over "walled gardens."

---

## 🎯 Our Objective
We believe that numbers speak louder than words. Our primary mission is to **gather as many members as possible** within our Game Jolt community to demonstrate to the Game Jolt administration that there is a high demand for this feature.

**The more we grow, the harder it is to ignore the future of the decentralized web.**

## 📜 Community Rules

To maintain a professional and effective advocacy group, all members must adhere to the following:

1.  **Stay On-Topic:** Keep discussions focused on ActivityPub, the Fediverse, and the technical or social benefits of decentralization for Game Jolt.
2.  **Be Respectful:** We are here to persuade and collaborate with Game Jolt, not to harass the developers. Maintain a constructive and polite tone.
3.  **No Spam:** Do not flood the community with unrelated links or repetitive content.
4.  **Spread the Word:** Share this community with fellow developers and gamers. Growth is our greatest tool for change.
